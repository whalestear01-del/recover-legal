What Recover is

Recover provides AI-powered call answering, lead capture, and follow-up services to home service contractors. If you're reading this, you've most likely contacted a business that uses Recover, or you're considering using Recover as a contractor.

If you're a contractor's customer

When you call or text a business that uses Recover, your conversation may be handled in part or in full by an AI assistant acting on that business's behalf. The assistant will identify itself as an AI assistant. Any commitments made during that conversation (appointment times, quotes, etc.) are made on behalf of, and are the responsibility of, the contractor you contacted — not Recover directly.

Text messaging

By providing your phone number to a contractor using Recover, you may receive text messages related to your inquiry or service (appointment reminders, follow-ups on quotes, etc.). Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

Call recording

Calls are recorded only with your verbal consent, given during the call. You may decline without affecting the service you receive.
